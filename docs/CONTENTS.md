# Sedbergh school news

SSN
Official Newspaper/Podcast of Sedbergh School Fuzhou (SSF)

Current Leader(s)
Sally Wu, Nina Karina Hoffmann

Founders
Sally Wu, Nina Karina Hoffmann

Sedbergh School News (or more commonly known as SSN) is the
official newspaper/podcast of Sedbergh School Fuzhou. Founded
in September 2026 by SSF G10 students Sally Wu and Nina Karina
Hoffmann, it first started off as an extracurricular project but soon
evolved into something much more.

This website provides biweekly/triweekly podcasts about either
school news, interviewing new students/teachers, discussing
school council-related events, talking to college counselors and
older students, audiobooks (where we read accordingly to
characters/narrator(s)), as well as weekly prompts. It is also a
‘newspaper’ as it offers more insight into SSF school life (also
includes lots of writing).

> Sally: “We believe this will provide younger students with more
> advice and help everyone tremendously.”

> Nina: “We think this will unite people together to practice their
> English, speaking, writing, and editing skills.”
